Contact Agriculture Calculator to suggest a tool, report an error, or ask about calculator formulas. We aim to make farm calculations clear and checkable.

Use this form to report a calculation issue, suggest an agriculture calculator, ask about an article, or share a reliable reference that may improve a tool. Please include the calculator name and the units or steps involved when reporting a possible error.

Do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal or farm account information. Your message will be processed by the form service so it can be sent to the contact email.
