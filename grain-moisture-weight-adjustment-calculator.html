  6. Change one input at a time to compare scenarios, and keep a copy of the assumptions with your farm records.

Formula and Calculation Method

Adjusted weight = measured weight × (100 − current moisture %) ÷ (100 − standard moisture %). This is a dry-matter balance.

How to Interpret Your Result

Grain moisture shrink uses dry-matter balance and may differ from elevator schedules or contract terms. Storage calculations estimate volume and do not certify structural safety or grain condition. Your result is most useful when the inputs match the same field, crop, unit basis, and time period. Review the entered values before comparing a result across fields or suppliers.

Check scale tickets, moisture-meter calibration, crop-specific test weight, contracts, drying charges, and storage manufacturer specifications.

Inputs That Affect the Grain Weight at Standard Moisture Calculator

The main inputs are harvested crop weight, current moisture and standard moisture. Use measured values where possible and note any assumptions you make for areas, rates, prices, losses, efficiency, or crop conditions.
