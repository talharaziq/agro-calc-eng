Research and Calculation Sources

A transparent review of agricultural calculator directories, university Extension resources, public soil tools, and unit-conversion references.

How the research was used

The calculator library was planned around tasks farmers look for online: planting and seed rates, nutrients and soil amendments, crop yield and value, spray calibration, irrigation, grain handling, livestock and forage, machinery, farm finance, and conversions. The review below checks broad calculator directories and selected official or university references to inform category coverage, page structure, and specific formulas.

Scope note: this is a category-level and reference-page review of the sources below, not an exhaustive audit of every individual calculator URL on each competitor site. No private keyword-volume data or guaranteed ranking outcomes are claimed. Each tool in this site still has its own SEO title, descriptive introduction, formula, input instructions, related links, and 13 FAQs written around its calculation and category.

Five agricultural calculator competitors and directories reviewed

AgroRates — all agricultural calculators

The directory groups tools into fertilizer and soil, seed and planting, planting dates, crop yield, livestock, irrigation and water, farm economics, and conversions. The useful pattern is category navigation and a popular-tool section. Agriculture Calculator applies similarly understandable groups while using a searchable, static HTML catalog and longer explanations on individual tools.

FarmCalcs — farming and agriculture calculators

The homepage groups tools into crop planning, soil and water, livestock, and farm finance, then surfaces popular calculators. This supports organizing the catalogue around farm tasks instead of an unfiltered alphabetical list. Our directory adds more categories and a client-side search/filter that works without a server.

AgriConverter — calculator and table index

The index provides filtering and groups conversions, yield, seed, and area tools, with worked examples and unit reference information. That pattern is useful for farmers who know a value or unit but not the page title. Our directory search checks tool titles, descriptions, categories, and search phrases.

FarmMaths — farming calculators

The site uses broad navigation for crops, livestock, machinery, irrigation, finance, soil, pasture, and farm infrastructure. Those are useful signals for building a taxonomy that covers a farm operation more fully than a small collection of seed and spray tools.

ToolFoundry — agriculture category

The category lists livestock, nutrient, spray, field capacity, grain drying, and farm finance tools with short descriptions. Descriptive task labels help people scan a large library. Agriculture Calculator keeps pages focused on one defined calculation and links related tasks.

University and public references

  • University of Minnesota Extension — Crop Calculators. Crop and nutrient tools include alfalfa, barley, corn, soybean, vegetable, and wheat resources; it is a reference for crop-specific nutrient calculator scope.
  • Iowa State University Extension — Ag Decision Maker Decision Tools. Farm budgets and crop, livestock, and whole-farm decision tools informed the farm-finance category.
  • Colorado State University Extension — Pesticide Sprayer Calibration Fundamentals. The sprayer nozzle tool uses the standard US boom-sprayer formula: GPA × MPH × nozzle spacing in inches ÷ 5,940 = GPM per nozzle. The page reports a review in August 2025.
  • Utah State University Extension — Irrigation Tools and Calculators. A reference for irrigation planning and decision-support categories.
  • USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service — Soils Tools. Useful starting point for soil information, surveys, and soil-related data.
  • National Institute of Standards and Technology — Guide to the SI, conversion factors. A public conversion reference for US customary and metric units.
  • NIST Handbook 44, Appendix C — General Tables of Units of Measurement. Includes references such as an acre equaling 43,560 square feet and standard US volume relationships.

On-page SEO choices

Each page has a distinct title and meta description, a descriptive H1, a plain-language intro, labeled input fields, formula text, instructions, example guidance, related calculators, an external reference where relevant, and calculator-specific FAQ content. The all-calculator directory is present as HTML in the delivered files, so the links are available without a client-side API. It also includes category filters and a browser search box.

These are technical and content foundations, not a promise of rank position. To improve the site after launch, verify the published domain, test every formula with known examples, use Search Console to observe actual queries and indexing, and update pages based on useful reader feedback and real search data.
